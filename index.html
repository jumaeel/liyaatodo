Getting started

How Liyaatodo works

A quick walkthrough of every part of the app — follow the steps below and you'll be tracking real progress in a couple of minutes.

1

Create a project

A project is a folder for related tasks — like Studies, Work, or Errands. Use the “New project…” box at the bottom of the left sidebar, type a name, and hit the + button. You can make as many as you like; switch between them by clicking their name.

2

Add your tasks

Open a project and use the Add task form. Give it a title, pick a priority (High / Medium / Low), an optional deadline, and an estimated time (type things like 30m, 2h, or 1h30m). You can also tag a task ★ Important and ⚡ Urgent — that powers the priority matrix in step 5. Tick the checkbox when it's done and your progress updates instantly.

3

Pick your “Today’s Focus”

Open Today’s Focus from the menu and choose up to 5 tasks you’ll finish today — pulled from any of your projects. Use the search box to find them fast. Tick one off here and it’s also completed in its original project. It's the daily shortlist that keeps you focused.

4

Watch your progress

The Dashboard is your command centre: total / completed / active / overdue counts, an overall completion gauge, a bar for every project, and a priority breakdown. It updates live as you check tasks off — so you can see momentum building across everything at a glance.

5

Prioritise with the Matrix

Inside a project, switch from List to Matrix view to see the Eisenhower Matrix: four boxes — Do (important & urgent), Schedule, Delegate, and Delete. Drag a task into a box to set its importance/urgency. It’s the fastest way to decide what actually deserves your attention.

6

Sign in to save & sync

Tap your avatar (top-right) → Account & sync → Continue with Google. Once signed in, everything saves to your account automatically and syncs across all your devices — finish a task on your phone and it’s updated on your laptop. Signed out, your data still saves in this browser.

Handy to know
  • Search at the top of any page jumps to matching tasks.
  • Works offline — it’s installable as an app from your browser’s “Add to Home Screen / Install” option.
  • Overdue tasks are flagged in red so nothing slips through.
  • Add a time estimate to any task and Liyaatodo totals up how much work each project needs.
  • Use the 🌙 / ☀️ button in the top bar to switch between dark and light mode.
  • Deleting a project removes its tasks too — you’ll be asked to confirm first.
