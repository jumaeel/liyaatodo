Getting started

How Liyaatodo works

A quick walkthrough of every part of the app — follow the steps below and you'll be tracking real progress in a couple of minutes.

Quick tour

tap a card to try it
1

Create & organise projects

A project is a folder for related tasks — like Studies, Work, or Errands. Use the “New project…” box at the bottom of the left sidebar, type a name, and hit the + button. Hover a project in the sidebar to rename ✏️ it, reorder ▲▼ it, or delete it. Click its name to open it — or click the Liyaatodo logo any time to jump back to the Dashboard.

2

Add & edit tasks

Open a project and use the Add task form. Give it a title, pick a priority (High / Medium / Low), a deadline (defaults to one week out), and an estimated time (type things like 30m, 2h, or 1h30m). Tag a task ★ Important and ⚡ Urgent to power the matrix in step 5. Need to change something later? Hit the ✏️ pencil on any task to edit every detail. Tick the checkbox when it's done and your progress updates instantly.

3

Pick your “Today’s Focus”

Open Today’s Focus and choose up to 5 tasks you’ll finish today — pulled from any of your projects (search to find them fast). Ticking one off here also completes it in its original project. The screen has a live clock and a “time left today” countdown, a fresh motivational quote every few minutes, and a ⛶ Fullscreen button for a distraction-free focus view.

4

Watch your progress

The Dashboard is your command centre: total / completed / active / overdue counts, an overall completion gauge, a bar for every project (with its total estimated time), and a priority breakdown. The Focus Matrix here spells out exactly what’s important & urgent for you and lists those tasks — tap one to jump straight to its project. Everything updates live as you check tasks off.

5

Prioritise with the Matrix

Inside a project, switch from List to Matrix view to see the Eisenhower Matrix: four boxes — Do (important & urgent), Schedule, Delegate, and Delete. Drag a task into a box to set its importance/urgency. It’s the fastest way to decide what actually deserves your attention.

6

Sign in to save & sync

Tap your avatar (top-right) → Account & sync → Continue with Google. Once signed in, everything saves to your account automatically and syncs across all your devices — finish a task on your phone and it’s updated on your laptop. Signed out, your data still saves in this browser.

Handy to know
  • The search bar appears inside a project and filters that project’s tasks live.
  • Edit any task with the ✏️ pencil — that’s also how you add a time estimate to older tasks.
  • Hover a project in the sidebar to rename, move ▲▼, or delete it. Click the logo to return to the Dashboard.
  • Add a time estimate to any task and Liyaatodo totals up how much work each project needs.
  • Today’s Focus has a live clock, a time-left-today countdown, rotating quotes, and a ⛶ Fullscreen focus mode.
  • Use the 🌙 / ☀️ button in the top bar to switch between dark and light mode.
  • Works offline and is installable as an app; overdue tasks are flagged in red. Deleting a project removes its tasks (you’ll confirm first).
